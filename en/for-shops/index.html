The problem

One piece, five honest numbers, and nobody explains the gap

A customer arrives with a figure in their head. It came from an insurance valuation, a shop window, or an aunt. It is almost never the number you are about to say out loud, and the next ten minutes go on explaining why.

The arithmetic underneath is not hard. It is just tedious, and three things go wrong in it every week.

01

Gross weight is not gold weight. A 12 g ring with stones can hold 5 g of metal.

02

Carat weight alone says nothing. A 1 ct diamond runs from about €700 to €12,000.

03

Treatment collapses value. A glass-filled ruby is worth less than the gold around it.

04

Lab-grown is everywhere. Chemically identical, and 10 to 20 % of the price.

How it works

Three inputs, one screen, about a minute

STEP 01

Enter the piece

Metal, hallmark, net weight without the stones. Stones by carat, natural or lab-grown. Loose stones with no metal at all are fine.

STEP 02

Prices arrive by themselves

The metal spot price is fetched live and refreshes on its own. Stone prices come from a wholesale matrix, and you can override any of it by hand.

STEP 03

Read the ladder

Five values side by side, from melt floor to new retail, so the difference you are explaining to a customer is on the screen instead of in your head.

Buying at auction

The hammer price is not the price

If you buy stock at auction, the app adds what the listing leaves out and returns the maximum you should bid, not the maximum you could.

Where it helps, and where it does not

The limits, before you find them yourself

+

Metal at the live spot price, any purity, net of the stones.

−

Brand premiums. A signed piece is worth more than its parts, and the app does not know by how much.

+

Diamonds by the four Cs, natural and lab-grown, at wholesale per carat.

−

Old cuts and antique work. No modern grade describes them properly.

+

All auction costs: premium, VAT on the premium, import VAT and duty, shipping.

−

Detecting treatment. It prices what you tell it the stone is.

+

Loose stones with no metal, and scrap with no stones.

−

Replacing a valuer. It is the arithmetic under the judgement, not the judgement.

Try it against a piece you have already valued

That is the only test worth running: put something through it where you already know what you paid, and see whether the number holds.
