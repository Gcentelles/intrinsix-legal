Intrinsix — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 July 2026

Intrinsix (“the app”, “we”) is a buyer-side calculator that helps you decide how much to bid on jewellery auction lots. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and your rights. We keep data collection to the minimum the service needs. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, we do not track you across apps, and we do not sell your data.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo create and sign in to your account (only needed to manage an Intrinsix Pro subscription; the free calculator needs no account).
Authentication tokens / user identifierTo keep you signed in and to tie your subscription and free-use allowance to your account.
Subscription & purchase statusTo unlock Intrinsix Pro. Payments are processed by Apple; we and RevenueCat receive the transaction/subscription status, not your card details.
Free-use countA single number recording how many free AI analyses you have used, so the free tier works across your devices.
Auction lot content you choose to importWhen you use AI import, the publicly visible content of the auction lot page you open (title, description text, listed specifications) is sent to our server and AI provider to extract structured specs. This is listing content, not your personal data, though it may incidentally contain seller information shown publicly on that page.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity. Requests to precious-metal and currency price providers carry no account data.

2. Who processes your data (subprocessors)

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseAccount authentication and database hosting.
RevenueCatSubscription management.
AnthropicAI extraction of lot specifications from imported listing content. Content sent via the Anthropic API is not used to train their models.
AppleApp distribution and subscription payments.
gold-api.com, frankfurter.dev, metals.devLive precious-metal spot and currency rates. No account data is sent.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the calculator and managing your subscription), for our legitimate interests (security and preventing abuse of the free tier), and, where required, with your consent. Intrinsix acts as the data controller for your account data.

4. Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account. Imported lot content is processed transiently to return the extracted specs and is not retained as a long-term profile about you.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app (Pro → Account → Delete account). EU/EEA and UK users may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below.

6. Data location and security

Data may be processed on servers located in the European Union and the United States. Access to your account is protected by row-level security so you can only see your own data.

7. Children

Intrinsix is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be reflected in the app.
